SOLVANE BAY is an original open-world crime game for desktop Chrome (keyboard + mouse): a sun-soaked low-poly coastal city you explore on foot and by car, as newcomer Nico Varela working up from Dex's Club Velvet to a marina heist.

- Every car carries a soft contact shadow with darker tyre patches, so it sits on road, ramp or lift even under roofs
- Procedural three.js city in a teal-and-coral colour script: each story act has its own grade (Act I morning mint, Act II golden hour, Act III dusk teal sky into a coral horizon), long teal shadows, an amber sun low over a turquoise sea; the LOW TIDE BLAZE preset (one place, render/colourScript.js) takes over at evening magic hour, in staged shots and on the title: deep teal overhead through coral to hot gold at the horizon, hot gold key with teal split-toned shadows, gold sand, sun glints on the sea, stronger contrast with lifted teal blacks
- Landmarks: the coral-red cable-stayed Causeway with two pylons and cable fans over the sea (floodlit from late afternoon: glowing coral legs with gold corner strips, marker lamps, a warm lamp bead every 24 m along the deck, cables brightening at dusk), the striped Point Wren light with a lamp glow and turning beam, Crown Tower (tallest glass tower in Crown Heights, gold crown crest that glows at dusk), the Old Town lagoon market on stilts under coral, amber and teal festoon bulb strings
- Busy streets, district by district (vehicles/trafficDensity.js): up to ~30 civilian cars and ~38 walkers around the player, scaled down on slower quality tiers, with each district's own mix (cabs and buses downtown, Calder box trucks and vans at the Port Calder docks, convertibles and buses on the Strand, sports cars in Neon Row)
- Street life: TIDE BUS minibuses that stop every couple of blocks while riders step off and board (vehicles/busRoutes.js); moto-taxis and courier delivery scooters on a shared two-wheeler lane module that pull in to the kerb for drop-offs (vehicles/laneRiders.js, deliveryScooters.js); Calder box trucks with courier liveries; chatting groups outside the shops, friends on the sand and joggers on the promenade that scatter when shooting starts (characters/streetCrowds.js); beach-goers, umbrellas, lifeguard towers and a volleyball net along the Strand
- Hand-written post-processing: HDR render target, controlled bright-pass bloom pyramid, ACES filmic tone mapping, the act grade from render/colourScript.js, vignette, speed fringe and an at-speed radial blur (builds past ~90 km/h, stronger on nitro) that only touches the outer frame (zero over the central ~60%, so the car and pursuers stay sharp) with thin gold speed lines at the edges
- Sleek extruded car models with clearcoat paint, tinted glass, spoked rims and glowing head/tail lights: VELOCE GT sports car, SBPD cruisers in a navy/teal livery with red/blue light bars, taxi, van, box truck, bus, SUV, compact, convertible
- Smooth low-poly people: rounded heads with faces and hair styles, open jackets with collars, hands and shoes; varied pedestrian outfits, SBPD officers in navy uniforms with caps and badges, Nico in a teal bomber over a white tee
- Third-person on foot (WASD, aim, SMG/pistol/shotgun/fists) and arcade driving with drift, damage, smoke, explosions, skid marks, chase camera with speed FOV
- Driving systems: NITRO (Shift or N while driving) with a draining/refilling tank, extra thrust, exhaust flames and FOV kick; vehicle CONDITION % from collision damage with engine smoke and fire when low; DRIFT xN chains that pay cash for sustained slides and NEAR MISS bonuses for threading past traffic at speed, shown as floating popups; counter-steering front wheels and tyre smoke
- Steal any car; lane-following traffic with signals; pedestrians that flee gunfire
- Wanted level 0-5: SBPD patrol cars that pursue and ram, roadblocks, officers on foot, a police helicopter, CUFFED / FLATLINED respawns; a new or raised wanted level holds for a 10 s dispatch window so the first units always arrive; harbour launches count as eyes on you (IN SIGHT)
- Police line of sight: units only see you with a clear sight line within range (IN SIGHT / SEARCHING tag), and heat only cools while nobody has eyes on you; roadblocks lay spike strips that shred your tyres
- Story, SOLVANE BAY: LOW TIDE: Nico Varela comes home on the night ferry to find his mother's diner taken by Viktor Brandt's Crown Syndicate. 8 chapters in 3 acts (steal, deliver, collect, shoot-outs, car chase, transmitter plant, yacht heist, live broadcast escape) with portraits and dialogue, a countdown to the Causeway Gala, and a chapter-complete card (beat, cash, rep, unlocks, turf change)
- Chapter 7 set piece, THE LEDGER: steal Brandt's Veloce GT, drive through the Marina Cala gate, walk pier nine and board his three-deck coral-and-cream yacht the CROWN JEWEL on foot, take out the deck guards, crack the safe for the ledger, then escape in the Skipjack speedboat with SBPD harbour launches ramming at your wake, under the Causeway to Sol at the Point Wren light
- Boats: water-only arcade physics (thrust, drag, stern slide, buoyancy bob, bow lift, roll into turns), a white wake ribbon and bow spray, boat-to-boat ramming and sinking, pursuit AI; F boards a boat at a pier and steps ashore only at a dock or jetty
- Chapter 8 finale, CAUSEWAY GALA: Tamsin reads the ledger live on TIDE FM while you drive the broadcast van up the new on-ramp onto the Causeway at dusk; an SBPD roadblock wall of cruisers with spike strips and flashing bars blocks the deck, AIR-1 sweeps its searchlight lane to lane and Captain Reyne's own car is on your bumper; ram the weak point (nitro, BLACKOUT, PATCH UP help) to send it flying and reach the Gala stage at the far pylon
- Territory: every district is held by the CROWN or the LOW TIDE crew; chapters flip them; HUD waterfront bar and city map tinted by holder
- Crew abilities unlocked by the story: Sol HOT SWAP (Z, a car delivered beside you), Tamsin BLACKOUT (X, police lose sight for 10 s), Wren PATCH UP (C, instant repair), with cooldown rings on the HUD crew cards
- Five weapon slots (fists, pistol, SMG, shotgun, thrown MOLOTOV that sets cars alight), HUD slot bar and a Tab weapon wheel; rep levels unlock guns at Calder Arms; Glow Coat Respray clears heat; health/armor pickups
- Crew & Chapters screen (J or pause menu): crew cards, act/chapter list, territory, days to the Gala, unlocks
- Driving juice: DRIFT xN chains, NEAR MISS, TRADE PAINT (hard contact with a cruiser, with a spray of sparks) and a COMBO header when stunts chain; wrecked cars go up in a bright fireball with burning debris, its warm smoke column rolling up out of the flames over half a second instead of popping in
- AIR-1 is a proper police helicopter: slim cabin with a glass canopy, long tail boom with a swept fin and tail rotor, skids, a pale spinning rotor disc, SBPD white with a teal belt and coral tail band; its nose searchlight sweeps a cool-white cone with a pool of light on the ground
- Gunfights on the water: harbour launch bow gunners fire bursts with tracers and white bullet splashes, Nico fires back over the stern with the gun in hand, and a holed launch sinks in a fireball and keeps burning on the water; the SBPD RESPONSE card lists the launches (HL-n)
- Wren's garage at Point Wren, an open-front workshop on the avenue (sign, tool wall, tool chest, tyres, paint shelf, a four-post drive-on lift, warm strip lights): drive in and press E, the car rolls up onto the lift's runways and the garage panel docks beside it with stat bars (acceleration, top speed, armour, nitro), engine / armour plating / nitro tank upgrades with prices, and six named respray colours; save/load covers chapters, territory, crew, weapons and garage
- Chapter 3 is a street gunfight with the Coral Kings at the Old Town lagoon market; every stall cooks on bottled gas, and a shot gas cage goes up in a fireball that hurts everyone close, burns for a few seconds and is restocked later; the blast is a crime SBPD answers in force
- Coming back from the map, pause menu or a shop gives 1.5 s of grace: an arrest in progress starts over and no hit lands in the first moment back
- "Tide Chart" HUD language: one card system for the HUD and every menu (deep-teal glass, cream keyline, coral accent stripe, condensed display numbers, mint money, gold stars). The full HUD keeps four clear corners around an open centre: mission card and SBPD response list top left, cash, large wanted stars, rep and turf top right, a large 250 px rotating minimap (GPS route, police car, launch and helicopter blips, wanted rim) with crew chips and vitals bottom left, the five-slot weapon bar, weapon and speedometer bottom right; the radio card shows only for a few seconds after a station change. V switches to a compact corners layout; illustrated portraits in the crew chips, dialogue and Crew & Chapters
- City map (M) drawn as a nautical chart: sounding contours and depth figures, lagoon basin, districts tinted by territory holder, landmark symbols, compass rose, badges for the next chapter, shops, garages, the hospital, SBPD HQ, speedboats, taxi fares and street races, the hidden packages still out there, a GPS route and a legend strip; beside it the career panel (ui/careerPanel.js): a Bay completion ring (story, packages, side jobs, territory), chapters ticked off, career stats tracked in play and saved (cars stolen, top wanted level, km driven and on foot, gang members down, cops shaken, taxi fares, races won, cash earned), the districts Low Tide holds, and three save slots (two manual and an autosave after every chapter; Continue loads the latest); pause menu, three synthesized radio stations
- Fixed-step simulation (1/60 s with an accumulator) so low frame rates never run in slow motion; boot shader warm-up and quality calibration so play never stalls on shader compiles; menu clicks never fire the gun
- No build step, no network, no binary assets: all geometry, textures and sound are generated at run time

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Checks (the tools drive headless Chromium through Playwright): `?selftest=1` (page title SELFTEST PASS/FAIL; also covers boarding the yacht, the speedboat, the Causeway roadblock, both finale chapter cards, the market gas bottles, the garage lift view and the resume grace; `node tools/run-selftest.mjs` runs it headless), `node tools/playtest.mjs` (plays the real game: New Game, walk, sprint, shoot, wanted level and police, steal a car, drive, street density, map, pause, resume; prints errors and fps), `node tools/take-screenshots.mjs [scene]` (renders the staged scenes listed in shots.json: the Strand chase cover, the street firefight, the map with the career panel and the sunset marina boat chase; one file per scene under game/src/shots/).
